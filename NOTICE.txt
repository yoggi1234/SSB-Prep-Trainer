Note on practice material
The WAT word sets and SRT situations included in this app are original
practice prompts adapted from widely published SSB preparation material, not
official board papers.

The "classic TAT set" (data/tat-classic) is user-supplied practice material,
imported at the owner's request from a third-party TAT PDF and the owner's own
picture folder. It is not covered by this project's licence, no rights are
claimed over it, and it should not be redistributed. The separately bundled
museum pictures (data/tat-images) remain public domain under CC0 1.0 with
their sources recorded in data/tat-images.json.
